Care that doesn't stop at the hospital door.

ClintonCare helps health practitioners identify patients at risk of being readmitted within 30 days of discharge.

The problem

Readmissions are hard on patients and expensive for hospitals, and many can be prevented with timely follow-up. The difficulty is knowing who needs that follow-up most.

What we do

Learn from past discharges

A machine learning model is trained on historical admission records.

Score each new patient

It weighs age, insurance type, region, admission type, department and length of stay.

Explain the result

Every prediction shows a risk level, so clinicians can see why a patient was flagged.

Decision support, not diagnosis. Predictions assist clinical judgment and never replace it. Patient data is handled confidentially.
